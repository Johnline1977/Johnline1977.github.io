Foldrr

Privacy

Foldrr is a self-help app for people who want to stop gambling. We collect as little as possible and never sell data.

What stays on your iPhone

Your quiz answers, streak, money and time estimates, urge sessions, check-ins and Crew list are stored only on your device. You can export or delete them in the app (Profile → Your data).

Crew notifications

When you alert your Crew, Foldrr stores a short record in Apple iCloud (CloudKit): your Crew code, the kind of alert and your first name. Buddies who joined your Crew get a notification. No amounts, sites or session content are shared. Text alerts are sent by you, from your own phone.

Blocker

The Safari blocker and Screen Time run on your device. Foldrr does not see what you browse or which apps you open.

Payments

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Foldrr does not receive your payment details.

Foldrr is not a medical device or treatment. Questions or a request to delete data: reply to your TestFlight invite or contact the developer through the App Store.
